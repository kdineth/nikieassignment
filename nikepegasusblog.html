Nike Pegasus 41 Review: Is It The Best Daily Trainer of 2026?

By Sarah Jenkins

Published February 26, 2026 • 6 min read

TL;DR: Quick Summary

What it is: A highly versatile, neutral daily running shoe.
Best for: Beginners, daily miles, recovery runs, and walking.
Key Updates: Upgraded to ReactX foam for 13% more energy return and a more breathable engineered mesh upper.
Price: £119.95.

Pros

  • 13% more energy return with new ReactX foam.
  • Highly breathable, redesigned engineered mesh upper.
  • Exceptional waffle-tread traction for wet conditions.
  • 43% lower carbon footprint in midsole manufacturing.

Cons

  • Not the lightest option for competitive race days.
  • Design evolution is visually subtle compared to the 40.

The Nike Pegasus line has been the undisputed king of daily running shoes for over four decades. When you have a legacy that spans 40 iterations, changing the formula is a risky move. However, with the release of the Nike Pegasus 41, Nike has made one of the most significant upgrades to the franchise in recent memory, focusing heavily on sustainability and energy return.

Whether you're training for your first couch-to-5K or logging high mileage for an upcoming marathon block, the question remains: does the Pegasus 41 live up to the legendary hype? We took it to the unpredictable streets of London to find out.

The Biggest Upgrade: ReactX Foam

The most noticeable change in the Pegasus 41 is the midsole. Nike has officially swapped out the traditional React foam for the much-anticipated ReactX foam. Originally debuted in the InfinityRN 4, ReactX offers a reported 13% increase in energy return compared to standard React foam, while cutting the carbon footprint of the midsole manufacturing process by an impressive 43%.

On the run, this translates to a ride that feels slightly softer upon initial impact but significantly bouncier during toe-off. Combined with the signature dual Zoom Air units (one in the forefoot, one in the heel), the shoe provides a responsive snap that makes picking up the pace feel completely effortless, even on tired legs.

Fit, Upper, and Lockdown

Nike listened closely to feedback regarding the previous model's breathability during hot summer runs. The Pegasus 41 features an entirely redesigned engineered mesh upper that addresses these concerns head-on.

  • Lighter, Breezier Feel: The mesh material is noticeably thinner and more porous without sacrificing the shoe's overall structural integrity.
  • Improved Dynamic Lockdown: A newly integrated midfoot band wraps securely around the arch. This prevents any heel slippage during tight corners or sudden changes in pace.
  • True to Size Fit: The toe box offers just enough room for natural toe splay as your feet swell during longer efforts, making it comfortable even for runners with slightly wider feet.

Outsole: Built for British Weather

One area where the Pegasus has always excelled is traction, and the 41 is no exception. The classic waffle-inspired rubber outsole has been slightly tweaked to offer more grip on slick pavements and light trails. During our testing in typical wet UK conditions, the shoe provided immense confidence, gripping wet asphalt without any noticeable slippage.

Who Should Buy The Pegasus 41?

If you are looking for a "do-it-all" shoe, this is it. It thrives on easy miles, long slow distances, and handles tempo workouts surprisingly well. It is highly recommended for:

  • Beginner Runners: It offers the perfect balance of cushioning and stability.
  • One-Shoe Rotations: If you only want to buy one pair of running shoes for all your workouts, the Pegasus remains the industry gold standard.
  • Everyday Wear: With its sleek profile, it transitions perfectly from the track to the coffee shop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Nike Pegasus 41 true to size?

Yes, the Nike Pegasus 41 fits perfectly true to size. We recommend ordering your standard running shoe size. The redesigned engineered mesh upper provides a comfortable, accommodating fit even for slightly wider feet.

What is the difference between Pegasus 40 and 41?

The primary difference is the midsole foam. The Pegasus 41 uses the newer, more responsive ReactX foam, which provides 13% more energy return and reduces the shoe's carbon footprint by 43% compared to the React foam used in the Pegasus 40.

Is the Pegasus 41 good for marathons?

While elite runners may prefer carbon-plated shoes like the Alphafly for race day, the Pegasus 41 is an excellent, durable choice for completing a marathon, especially for beginners or those prioritizing comfort over pure speed.
