Nike Pegasus 41 Review: Is It The Best Daily Trainer of 2024?

Published June 5, 2024 • 6 min read

What it is: A highly versatile, neutral daily running shoe.
Best for: Beginners, daily miles, recovery runs, and walking.
Key Updates: Upgraded to ReactX foam and full-length Zoom Air for a more powerful ride.
Price: £129.95.

Pros

  • Superior energy return with new ReactX foam.
  • Highly breathable, redesigned engineered mesh upper.
  • Exceptional waffle-tread traction for wet conditions.
  • Familiar, reliable fit that fans of the franchise love.

Cons

  • Not the lightest option for competitive race days.

The Nike Pegasus line has been the undisputed king of daily running shoes for over four decades. When you have a legacy that spans 40+ iterations, changing the formula is a risky move. However, with the release of the Nike Pegasus 41, Nike has crafted a powerful workhorse that delivers the most Pegasus ride to date.

Whether you're training for your first couch-to-5K or logging high mileage for an upcoming marathon block, the question remains: does the Pegasus 41 live up to the legendary hype? We took it to the unpredictable streets of London to find out.

The Anatomy: ReactX Foam & Zoom Air

The most noticeable change in the Pegasus line recently is the midsole evolution. The Pegasus 41 utilizes the much-anticipated ReactX foam paired with full-length Zoom Air. This combination offers an incredibly responsive and plush ride.

On the run, this translates to a ride that feels slightly softer upon initial impact but significantly bouncier during toe-off. The shoe provides a responsive snap that makes picking up the pace feel completely effortless, even on tired legs.

Fit, Upper, and Lockdown

Nike listened closely to feedback regarding breathability during hot summer runs. The Pegasus 41 features an entirely redesigned engineered mesh upper that addresses these concerns head-on.

  • Lighter, Breezier Feel: The mesh material is noticeably thinner and more porous without sacrificing the shoe's overall structural integrity.
  • Improved Dynamic Lockdown: A newly integrated midfoot band wraps securely around the arch. This prevents any heel slippage during tight corners or sudden changes in pace.
  • True to Size Fit: The toe box offers just enough room for natural toe splay as your feet swell during longer efforts, making it comfortable even for runners with slightly wider feet.

Outsole: Built for British Weather

One area where the Pegasus has always excelled is traction, and the 41 is no exception. The classic waffle-inspired rubber outsole has been optimized to offer maximum grip on slick pavements and light trails.

Unmatched Grip

During our testing in typical wet UK conditions, the shoe provided immense confidence, gripping wet asphalt without any noticeable slippage. Perfect for year-round training.

Who Should Buy The Pegasus 41?

If you are looking for a "do-it-all" shoe, this is it. It thrives on easy miles, long slow distances, and handles tempo workouts surprisingly well. It is highly recommended for:

  • Beginner Runners: It offers the perfect balance of cushioning and stability.
  • One-Shoe Rotations: If you only want to buy one pair of running shoes for all your workouts, the Pegasus remains the industry gold standard.
  • Everyday Wear: With its sleek profile, it transitions perfectly from the track to the coffee shop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Nike Pegasus 41 true to size?

Yes, the Nike Pegasus 41 fits perfectly true to size. We recommend ordering your standard running shoe size. The redesigned engineered mesh upper provides a comfortable, accommodating fit even for slightly wider feet.

What is the difference between Pegasus 40 and 41?

The primary difference is the refined midsole and upper integration. The Pegasus 41 continues to utilize the highly responsive ReactX foam but pairs it with an updated full-length Zoom Air setup for a more unified, powerful ride compared to the 40.

Is the Pegasus 41 good for marathons?

While elite runners may prefer carbon-plated shoes like the Alphafly for race day, the Pegasus 41 is an excellent, durable workhorse choice for completing a marathon, especially for beginners or those prioritizing comfort and familiarity over pure speed.
